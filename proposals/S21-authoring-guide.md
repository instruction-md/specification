# S21 — Authoring guide, cheat sheet and delivered samples

**Type:** informative · **Status:** accepted (owner, 2026-09-25) · **Raised by:** specification authoring review, 2026-09-25

## Motivation

The specification is written for implementers. A writer needs to learn the
format in the order they will use it, see what the model receives, and have a
page to keep open.

## Content

- `guide/README.md` — levels of adoption (L0 plain Markdown → L1 rules and
  examples → L2 parameters, variants, includes and references → L3 machinery),
  the anatomy of a good instruction, patterns and anti-patterns, choosing a form.
- `guide/cheatsheet.md` — every construct on one page, with its delivered form.
- `samples/delivered/<sample>.txt` — what each sample delivers with its
  declared defaults, regenerated from the reference implementation.

Nothing here is normative.
